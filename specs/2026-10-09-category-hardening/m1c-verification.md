## Stateful trigger coverage follow-up

Review correctly identified that A–P did not establish the newly explicit stateful trigger guarantees. The published case table now also includes **Q** (initial armed observation, including an already-above record count), **R** (no refire on an unchanged count after success), **S** (rearm below the threshold and fire on a later crossing), and **T** (interval initial dormancy, baseline, completion-time reset and no reset on phase re-entry). All four link to MEM-008 and distinguish Runner observations from validator rejection. The rule index links these cases, and document checks require all twenty case IDs.

Two direct Harness regressions cover both `record_count` and `capacity`: `memory_lifecycle_count_triggers_start_armed_on_initial_boundary_observation` and `memory_lifecycle_count_triggers_do_not_refire_while_count_is_unchanged`. They seed real scoped records without prior trigger history, drive the existing post-write lifecycle entry point, observe successful output and trigger events, and verify retained source counts. The second repeats the observation three times and expects eligibility `[true, false, false]`, unchanged output count and disarmed completed state. They do not fabricate completed trigger state or expose private methods for tests. The lifecycle suite was rerun after these additions: **30 passed**, exit 0. The first test uses one observation; the second uses three, and both exercise record-count and capacity operations.

Existing rearm/dormancy/interval restart tests supply complementary implementation evidence. These checks do not claim that an independent Runner has passed the published cases or that M2A's executable conformance corpus is complete.
